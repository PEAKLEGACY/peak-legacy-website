A door can look perfect in a showroom and still be the wrong choice for your opening. Check these five things before you pay.

### 1. Opening size
Confirm the opening dimensions before selecting the door. Do not rely on a rough visual estimate.

### 2. Door swing
Check which direction the door needs to open and whether that works with the room, furniture and circulation.

### 3. Frame requirement
Ask whether you need a new frame, whether an existing frame can be used, and what finish you want around the opening.

### 4. Finish and exposure
Interior and exterior doors can face very different conditions. Consider moisture, sunlight, security and expected use.

### 5. Hardware
Handles, locks, hinges and other hardware affect both function and appearance. Plan them together instead of treating them as an afterthought.
