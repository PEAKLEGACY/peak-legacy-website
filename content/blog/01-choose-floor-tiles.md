Choosing tiles is not only about picking the colour you like. The right tile should also work for the room, the amount of traffic and the maintenance you are prepared to handle.

### 1. Start with the room
A living room, kitchen, bathroom and outdoor area do not have the same requirements. Think about water exposure, slip resistance, cleaning and foot traffic before you fall in love with a finish.

### 2. Look at the size and layout
Large-format tiles can make a space feel more open, while smaller formats can work well for detailed patterns and some bathroom layouts. Always consider the room dimensions and the final joint pattern.

### 3. Buy enough — but don't guess
Measure the area first, then allow for cuts and breakages. For a quotation, send us the approximate square metres and the type of space so we can guide you on the next step.

### 4. Take a sample home when possible
Lighting changes how a tile looks. A finish that looks one way in the showroom can look different in daylight or warm indoor lighting.
