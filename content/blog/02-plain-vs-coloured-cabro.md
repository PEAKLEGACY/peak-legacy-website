Both plain and coloured cabro can produce a strong paved surface. The bigger question is how you want the finished compound to look and how much design detail you need.

### Plain cabro
Plain paving gives you a cleaner, simpler appearance and can work well where the goal is a straightforward driveway, walkway or compound finish.

### Coloured cabro
Coloured cabro gives you more room to create patterns, borders and visual zones. It can make the paving itself part of the landscape design.

### Don't forget the installation
The final result depends on more than the cabro colour. Ground preparation, the bedding layer, levels, drainage, pattern and finishing all matter.
